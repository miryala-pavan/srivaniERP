Margin vs Markup — Difference
Margin = (Sell − Cost) / Sell × 100
Markup = (Sell − Cost) / Cost × 100

Cost ₹80, Sell ₹100 → Margin 20%, Markup 25%
20% margin ≠ 20% markup. Always use margin for pricing.

ITC (Input Tax Credit) Note
If GST registered: your actual cost = base price only (not incl GST) because you claim GST back as ITC.

If composition scheme: your cost = full invoice amount (incl GST) — no ITC claim.

Always calculate margin on the net cost to you.
